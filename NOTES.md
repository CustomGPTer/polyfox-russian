PolyFox Russian 1.0.0

The first version of PolyFox Russian, a Russian course of its own, made to the same standard as PolyFox French.

- The whole course, from the alphabet to C2: 180 units, 2,130 words and about 2,460 sentences, every one spoken.
- Five alphabet units before A1, with a chart of all 33 letters and a spoken word for each.
- English letters under every word at A1, behind a tap at A2, gone from B1. Stress marks up to the end of B1, ordinary print from B2. You never type a stress mark, and е is accepted for ё.
- PolyFox's own Russian keyboard, with each letter near its English sound-alike, or your phone's keyboard at one tap.
- Tables for 390 verbs with their aspect partners and 1,004 nouns in all six cases.
- About 1,000 other correct wordings accepted; a wrong answer says why (case, gender, number, past-tense gender, не).
- Speaking checked on the phone by Whisper Small. Eighteen conversations, 18 stories, a grammar book of 363 notes, dictation, level exams, up to five people and a backup you keep.

PolyFoxRussian.apk is about 318 MB. Android 8 or later. No internet permission.
