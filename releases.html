1.0.0 26 November 2026

The first version of PolyFox Russian, a Russian course of its own, made to the same standard as PolyFox French.

  • The whole course, from the alphabet to C2: 180 units, 2,130 words and about 2,460 sentences, every one spoken by two men’s voices.
  • Five alphabet units before A1: the letters you already know, the look-alikes that trick you, the new shapes, stress and the two signs with no sound, with a chart of all 33 letters and a spoken word for each.
  • English letters (privét) under every word at A1, behind a tap at A2, gone from B1. Stress marks on every word up to the end of B1, then ordinary Russian print from B2. You never type a stress mark, and е is accepted for ё.
  • PolyFox’s own Russian keyboard, with each letter near its English sound-alike, or your phone’s own keyboard at one tap.
  • The six cases come in step by step, and verbs are taught with their aspect partner. Tables for 390 verbs (present or perfective future, future, past by gender, imperative) and 1,004 nouns in all six cases, singular and plural. Tap any word, then all forms.
  • Typing is fair: different word orders, numbers as digits, and a man’s or a woman’s form (я был, я была) are accepted: about 1,000 other correct wordings in all.
  • A wrong answer says why: the wrong ending and the case, gender or number it should be, a past tense in the wrong gender, or a missing or extra не.
  • Speaking is checked on the phone by Whisper Small. A different number or a missing не does not pass. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can still mark yourself.
  • Eighteen conversations, from the café to a radio interview, and 18 stories read aloud, from a new cat to the Firebird.
  • A grammar book of all 363 notes, reviews timed by FSRS, the method behind Anki, dictation, level exams, up to five people and a backup you keep.
